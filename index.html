ER wait-time project · interactive prototype

H.A.L.O.

A live wait screen for the emergency waiting room. The kiosk tower and the phone page show the same information and share one live wait list.

Things to try

  • Press Call for help on either screen. The kiosk sounds an alarm.
  • Tap a place on the kiosk map for directions.
  • Play the video on the kiosk.
  • Tap the glowing phone pad on the kiosk. The page is sent to the phone.
  • Check in a patient at the front desk and watch where their triage level puts them.
  • Tap a ticket to see why its wait changed. The list also updates on its own.

Sound plays only after you tap. All tickets, times and places are examples.
